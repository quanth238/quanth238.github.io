About

I am a Master of Science student in Computer Science at VinUniversity and a Research Assistant at the Center for AI Research, supervised by Asst. Prof. Binh T. Nguyen. My research focuses on learning compact 3D representations through optimization and probabilistic machine learning.

I work at the intersection of 3D reconstruction, neural rendering, and generative modeling. My recent work studies structural learning for 3D Gaussian Splatting and geometry-aware methods for reconstruction and visual SLAM.

Previously, I was a Research Assistant at the Cognitive Robotics Lab, University of Arkansas, supervised by Asst. Prof. Tuan Dang. There, I proposed and developed TriaGS and co-first-authored research on geometry-aware online mapping.

For research collaborations, please get in touch.
